🧱 Block Stacker

Goal: Stack 10 blocks on a single spot as fast as you can!

How to play:

  • Press on a block to place the red selector.
  • Press F to place a block at the selected spot. you may also press G to delete a block.
  • The timer starts when you place the first block.
  • Once the stack hits height 10, the timer stops and shows your total time.
  • Press R to reset and play again!
Please use a browser that supports "canvas"
